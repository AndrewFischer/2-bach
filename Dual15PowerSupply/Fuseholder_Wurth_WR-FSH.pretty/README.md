# Wurth_WR-FSH_696105003002.pretty

KiCad 10 footprint library for Wurth Elektronik WR-FSH fuseholder, part 696105003002.

Footprint: `Wurth_WR-FSH_696105003002.kicad_mod`

Geometry:
- Fuse-link: 5 x 20 mm
- Body outline: 24.4 mm x 9.4 mm nominal
- Electrical contacts: pins 1 and 2, plated THT, finished drill Ø1.5 mm
- Contact pitch: 15.0 mm, pads at X = -7.5 mm and X = +7.5 mm
- Centre snap-in mounting pin: circular NPTH Ø2.6 mm, no copper
- Origin: centre of the snap-in mounting pin
- View convention: component-side PCB layout

Notes:
- The Ø1.5 mm contact hole is intended as the finished plated hole size. Board fabrication should compensate for plating.
- The centre Ø2.6 mm hole is mechanical only and intentionally unnumbered.
- Print 1:1 and test-fit the actual fuseholder before board fabrication.
